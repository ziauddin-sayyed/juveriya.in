Who we are

One contractor for interiors, civil work, fabrication & repair

Juveriya Interior Decorator is a building-restoration and interior-contracting firm working across many metro cities of India. Industrial and logistics majors call us for complete office setups, warehouse fit-outs, racking, fencing and electrical work — while housing societies, hotels, clubs and clinics call us for structural repair, waterproofing and painting.

Our strength is practical: measured, itemised scopes, in-house execution teams, and lasting solutions for the maintenance, innovation and expansion of built-up environments.

How we work

From site visit to handover in five steps

Our standard working method, as followed on every job we take up.

Site visit & measurement

We inspect the site, take measurements and note constraints such as power, water and access.

Detailed scope of work

You receive a measured, itemised scope — quantities and specifications agreed up front.

Confirmed work order

Work starts on a written purchase order; timelines are committed from that date.

Execution

Our teams execute civil, fabrication, electrical and finishing work with site supervision.

Handover

Joint final measurement and snag-free handover of the finished work.
