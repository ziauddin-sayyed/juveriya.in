Our story

Built on quality, precision & customer satisfaction

Juveriya Interiors was established in Mumbai in 1990 by Mukhtar Gulsher Khan. What began as an interior and finishing outfit has grown into a multi-disciplinary contracting firm handling turnkey interiors, fabrication, electrical and civil works.

Today the firm is owned and managed by Mukhtar Gulsher Khan and Sufiyan Khan (sales & business development), with execution teams of carpenters, fabricators, electricians and civil workers deployed to sites across Maharashtra, Gujarat, Haryana, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, Assam, Kerala and beyond.

Our philosophy is simple: offer real value, work closely with the client, and stand behind the built environment we create, maintain and expand.
