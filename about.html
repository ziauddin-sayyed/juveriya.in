Our story

Built on quality, precision & customer satisfaction

Juveriya Interior Decorator Co. was established in Mumbai in 1990 by Mukhtar Gulsher Khan. What began as an interior and finishing outfit has grown into a multi-disciplinary contracting firm handling turnkey interiors, steel fabrication, electrical and civil works.

Today the firm is managed by Sufiyan Khan (sales & business development) and Sayed Imran (project head), with execution teams of carpenters, fabricators, electricians and civil workers deployed to sites across Maharashtra, Gujarat, Haryana, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, Assam, Kerala and beyond.

Our philosophy is simple: offer real value, work closely with the client, and stand behind the built environment we create, maintain and expand.
