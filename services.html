Every service, scoped in detail

Every service below starts with a site visit and a detailed, itemised scope of work — measured quantities and specifications agreed before work starts. Representative past scopes are noted under each service. Pan-India project execution, with key locations in Bhiwandi, Delhi NCR and across India.

01

Turnkey Office & Warehouse Interiors

Complete interior setups for logistics, industrial and commercial clients — from bare shell to ready-to-occupy.

  • Office blocks with cabins, conference rooms, reception, canteen, locker & server rooms
  • Aluminium partitions, modular/false ceilings, workstations and modular furniture
  • Gypsum and grid false ceilings with concealed lighting provisions
  • Cold cabins with anti-static flooring (e.g. 3,500 sq ft facility, Bhiwandi)
  • Turnkey offices executed at Bhiwandi, Chakan, Vadgaon-Pune, Gandhidham, Surat, Bhopal, Kochi, Guwahati & more
  • Pan-India turnkey project execution from Mumbai — key locations Bhiwandi, Delhi NCR and across India
02

Mezzanine Floors

Steel mezzanine floors that add usable capacity over an existing floor area.

  • Structural steel mezzanine decks for storage, offices and production areas
  • Floor decking, staircases, handrails and edge protection
  • Finishes to match the surrounding fit-out — flooring, partitions and lighting
  • Past scope: mezzanine floor layout and works for Mahindra Logistics, Chinmanpada
03

Waterproofing & Painting

Leak-proofing and protective coatings for terraces, facades and interiors.

  • Exterior waterproofing without breaking existing surfaces
  • Membrane application on large terraces and industrial roofs
  • Interior/exterior painting, badminton-court and floor coatings, floor polishing
  • Two- and multi-coat emulsion paint systems over prepared plaster and putty
  • Past scopes: Everest Chamber Malabar Hill, Crystal Enclave, Sky Industries
04

Steel Fabrication & Fencing

Heavy fabrication for terminals, warehouses and industrial plots.

  • Pre-engineered sheds and gate structures (7,000 sq ft gate shed, APM Terminals Panvel)
  • TCT-sheet and wire-mesh fencing for yards and warehouses (1,000+ sq m scopes)
  • Weighbridge and gate civil works at container terminals
  • Cladding, gates, grills and custom MS/SS fabrication
05

Warehouse Racking & Relocation

Storage systems supplied, installed, shifted and dismantled.

  • Heavy-duty and slotted-angle racks with installation and alignment
  • Warehouse shifting: racks, stock-line equipment and office fittings moved between facilities
  • Dismantling of partitions, ceilings, workstations, AC units and electrical fits for relocation
  • Executed across Bhiwandi, Bengaluru, Ahmedabad and other hub locations
06

Electrical Materials, Works, CCTV & Networking

Power and data backbone for warehouses and offices.

  • Supply and installation of electrical materials — cables, boards, switches and lighting
  • HT/LT cabling, distribution boards, changeover switches and terminations
  • Complete wiring with switchboards, MCB distribution boards and earthing
  • LED high-bay, flood, panel and tube lighting with testing & commissioning
  • CCTV, beam detectors, networking and access-system wiring
  • Past scopes: CEVA Kalher, TVS facilities, Rhenus Vadodara/Chennai, UIDAI Colaba
07

Plumbing, Cladding & Safety Surfaces

Specialist finishing lines listed in our company profile, delivered as part of turnkey scopes.

  • Plumbing installation and repair for commercial premises
  • ACP / panel cladding for interior partitions and cold cabins
  • Home-automation provisions (lighting, access and control wiring)
  • Anti-slip treatment for wet and high-traffic floor areas
08

Rolling Shutters, Gates, Trolleys & Loading Docks

Fabricated access and material-handling steelwork for warehouses, factories and yards.

  • Rolling shutters for warehouses, factories and retail units
  • Heavy-duty sliding and swing gates for industrial plots and yards
  • Heavy-duty trolleys and custom material-handling fabrications
  • Loading-dock fabrication, including dock-pit works at warehouses (e.g. Mahindra Logistics, Bhiwandi)
  • Aluminium sliding gate works executed for logistics clients in Bhiwandi
09

Heavy-Duty Vinyl & Epoxy Flooring

Durable floor systems for high-traffic commercial and industrial areas.

  • Heavy-duty vinyl flooring for offices, corridors and work areas
  • Epoxy floor coatings for industrial floors, workshops and process areas
  • Anti-static, anti-slip and protective floor-coating systems
  • Surface preparation, floor polishing and finishing
10

Safety Doors, Paneling & Molding

Entrances, wall treatments and ceilings that finish a space properly.

  • Safety doors and wooden/glass doors with frames, closers, locks and stoppers
  • Wall paneling and molding designs for cabins and receptions
  • Gypsum false ceilings and grid ceilings for offices and institutions
  • Past scopes: wooden and glass partitions with doors for UIDAI, Colaba; cabin paneling
